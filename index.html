Built to show the full analytical decision cycle.

This independent portfolio project uses generated synthetic banking data. Financial figures are computed from the supplied FactFinancials dataset, customer churn uses the opening-eligible monthly cohort, and transaction metrics come from FactTransactions. No actual bank, customers, or production deployment is represented.
